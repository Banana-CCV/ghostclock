## 2. Detailed Technical Critiques

### 2.1 Why Open-Source ENF Matchers (`nate-griff/enf`) Fail in Forensic Settings
Our code review and empirical unit test ([`work/W10/test_matcher_unit.py`](../experiments/w10_matcher_and_null_calibration/test_matcher_unit.py)) identified four fatal flaws in `nate-griff/enf/enf_compare.py` (`work/W05/repos/nate-griff-enf/enf_compare.py`, not included in this repository):
1. **Interior `NaN` Dropout Collapse (Line 36)**: `clip_series.dropna()` removes missing/low-SNR windows and concatenates the remaining samples without preserving their time offsets. On a 300 s real GB NESO trace with a 40 s interior dropout gap, `nate-griff/enf` shifts the second half of the trace by 40 s and misses the true timestamp by **$-30,907\,\text{s}$** ($r = 0.6541$), whereas [`work/W10/matcher.py`](../experiments/w10_matcher_and_null_calibration/matcher.py) masks the gap with $w_t = 0$ on the uniform time grid and recovers the exact timestamp (**$0\,\text{s}$ error**, $r = 0.9295$, $p_{\text{emp}} = 1.34 \times 10^{-6}$).
2. **Uncentered `10 mHz` Proximity Penalty (Lines 107–128)**: Computing `pct_within_10mHz` without subtracting the mean difference $(\bar{x}_w - \bar{y}_w(\tau))$ penalizes ordinary consumer crystal-oscillator offsets ($\pm 100\,\text{ppm} \approx \pm 5\,\text{mHz}$ at $50\,\text{Hz}$).
3. **Sidelobe Confusion in Peak Separation (Line 122)**: Setting `min_time_separation = 5.0 s` is narrower than the power-grid autocorrelation width ($\tau_c \approx 15\text{–}30\,\text{s}$), so adjacent points on the main correlation peak are misclassified as competing candidate matches.
4. **Zero Look-Elsewhere Calibration**: Returning top-$K$ candidates without calibrating for the number of independent trials $M_{\text{eff}}$ across a 24-hour or multi-day search window yields a **100% False Positive Rate** on `H0` (no-ENF) and wrong-day negative controls.

### 2.2 Why Naive i.i.d. Fisher-$z$ Testing Hallucinates Significance (`p = 6.65e-58` on Wrong Day)
In a 24-hour search window ($86,400\,\text{s}$) with a $D = 300\,\text{s}$ query trace cut from real GB NESO data and matched against the **wrong day**, the maximum spurious correlation across all $86,101$ lags reaches $r_{\max} = 0.7607$.
- A naive i.i.d. Fisher-$z$ test using nominal sample count $N = 300$ computes $z = \text{atanh}(0.7607)\sqrt{297} = 17.20$, yielding $p_{\text{iid}} = 6.65 \times 10^{-58}$ (`MATCH_SUPPORTED` — a catastrophic forensic false positive).
- Why? Because GB grid frequency has a $1/f$-like autocorrelation spectrum ($\rho(1\,\text{s}) \approx 0.97$, integral timescale $\tau_{\text{int}} \approx 35\text{–}55\,\text{s}$), reducing Bartlett's effective degrees of freedom from $N = 300$ to $N_{\text{eff}} \approx 6\text{–}10$, while maximizing over $M_{\text{eff}} \approx 2,500$ independent candidate locations across 24 hours shifts the extreme-value null distribution to $[0.60, 0.78]$.
- [`work/W10/matcher.py`](../experiments/w10_matcher_and_null_calibration/matcher.py) combines Bartlett's $N_{\text{eff}}$ with an empirical time-reversed reference surrogate distribution in Fisher-$z$ space, correctly assigning $p_{\text{emp}} = 0.0987 > 0.015$ and abstaining (`INCONCLUSIVE`).

### 2.3 Diagnosing Single-Harmonic Contamination in `work/C1/enf.py` vs Inter-Harmonic Consensus (`W06` / `W10`)
When auditing `work/C1/gt_w16.json`, we noticed that several clean 10-minute recordings in `ENF-WHU` (`H1/003.wav`, `H1/004.wav`, `H1/005.wav`) had near-zero correlation with `H1_ref` in C1's table (`rec_ref_r_at_0 = -0.203` for `003`, `+0.118` for `004`, `+0.068` for `005`), even though `W06` ([`work/W06/audio_enf.py`](../experiments/w06_audio_extractor/audio_enf.py)) achieved $r > 0.99$ on those exact same recordings.
- **Root Cause**: In [`work/C1/enf.py`](../experiments/c1_enfwhu_unseen_holdout/enf.py), C1 maps all 7 harmonics onto a common grid weighted linearly by `snr_frame` (`comb += (g / med[:, None]) * snr_frame[:, j:j + 1]`), extracts a single `coarse` peak, and then restricts every harmonic's fine peak `fk` to $\pm 2$ FFT bins around `k * coarse`. Whenever one harmonic band contains a narrowband acoustic/electronic interferer with high peak-to-median ratio, it dominates `comb`, drags `coarse` away from the true ENF, and blinds all other harmonics.
- **Resolution**: Picking peaks **independently** per harmonic band $k \in \{1..7\}$ and combining them via **inter-harmonic temporal trajectory correlation consensus** (requiring consenting harmonics to share correlated fluctuations over time) eliminates single-harmonic contamination. We recommend that the final integrated pipeline use inter-harmonic trajectory consensus (`W06` / `W10`) rather than pre-peak linear SNR summation.
